# Product Vision & Principles

## Long-term target

SportsBrain should become a premium, trustworthy sports intelligence and betting-signal product that a knowledgeable paying external user can use without developer assistance.

## Principles

- correctness before feature count
- current market truth before attractive UI
- safety invariants before convenience
- explicit provenance before inferred identity
- fail closed for money/actionability
- degrade visibly for informational failures
- privacy by architecture, not by copy
- deterministic releases and rollback
- model evidence and calibration before rollout
- monitoring validates product outcomes, not only cron activity.

## Non-goal

Do not chase 10.0 by adding features. Reaching 7+ requires removal of known reliability/security/operational weaknesses.
