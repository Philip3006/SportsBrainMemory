# FND-20260814-003 — Cancellation does not share placement durable-ACK semantics

## Problem / evidence

Cancellation may mutate the local ledger and clear cancel requests without the same canonical remote durability proof required for accepted placements.

## Failure / impact

Cancellation can disappear or revert after ephemeral runner loss.

## Required closure

Put cancellation in same durable mutation transaction or independently gate cancel ACK on remote durability.
