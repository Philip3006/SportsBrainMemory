# FND-20260814-002 — Retry semantics still conflate zero bankroll with outage

## Problem / evidence

The consumer now has explicit retry IDs and avoids ACK for transient failures. However `_row_from_bet()` classifies `bankroll is None or bankroll <= 0` as RETRY.

`None` is unavailable authority and retryable. A successfully computed finite bankroll `<=0` is an authoritative risk state, not an infrastructure outage. The pending stake cannot satisfy the 5% rule and must not wait in KV to become valid later.

## Failure / impact

A stale/permanently invalid money intent can accumulate and later be reconsidered under changed risk state.

## Required closure

`None`/lookup exception → RETRY. Finite authoritative `<=0` → permanent REJECT. Preserve open-count exception as RETRY. Test None, zero, negative and positive states.
