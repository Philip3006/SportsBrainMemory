# DEC-0006 — No auto-betting

## Decision

SportsBrain may recommend/log explicit user actions but may not autonomously place real bets.

## Consequence

This decision is part of the canonical CEO governance layer. A contradictory implementation or prompt must be treated as drift until the decision is explicitly superseded.
