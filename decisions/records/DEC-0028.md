# DEC-0028 — Top-5 Timing and Atomic Batch Semantics

## DECISION

Top-5 planning uses `INITIAL` at 22–26 hours and `REFINEMENT` at 60–120
minutes. Actionable odds freshness is at most 900 seconds. A five-fixture
batch is atomic at the governed publication boundary. The legacy 0–3 hour path
is not a production substitute, and closing odds remain a benchmark only.

## CONSEQUENCE

Any canary, publication, or activation proposal must prove these semantics in
the exact artifact and current source; timing labels alone do not authorize
execution.
