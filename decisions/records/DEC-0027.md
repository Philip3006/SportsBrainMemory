# DEC-0027 — Operational Truth Hierarchy

## DECISION

Production/runtime evidence outranks fresh main, exact-head CI, canonical
datasets, approved decisions, operational memory, historical notes, and
hypotheses in that order. Runtime/data SHA and source-release SHA remain
distinct. Conflicts are recorded and weaker claims are marked superseded or
unverified rather than rewritten.

## CONSEQUENCE

The home page is a navigation layer. Current state is intentionally ephemeral;
historical records retain their original evidence and timestamps.
