# DEC-0029 — Research Promotion and Negative Evidence

## DECISION

Elo is the strongest validated Nations League baseline recorded in the current
research gate, but research completion does not promote a model to production.
Causal GBT, safe competition context, and historical squad features remain
non-promoted unless their exact, reproducible evidence is independently
verified. Negative evidence is a first-class artifact.

## CONSEQUENCE

Builders must consult [[findings/NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE]] before proposing a repeat
experiment or treating a branch-only result as a production fact.
